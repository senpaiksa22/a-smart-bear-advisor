# البوصلة الاستراتيجية ليست خريطة تفصيلية: كيف تقود وسط الضباب
### A compass is not a map

> **الملخص التنفيذي:** يقدم جيسون كوهين في هذا المقال رؤية نقدية وعملية حول 'البوصلة الاستراتيجية ليست خريطة تفصيلية: كيف تقود وسط الضباب'، مفككاً التحديات الشائعة التي تواجه رواد الأعمال بأسلوب يجمع بين المنطق الرياضي واقتصاديات السوق.
> **المحور التصنيفي:** استكشاف العملاء والمقابلات وشخصية المشتري المثالي
> **الرابط الأصلي للمقال:** [https://longform.asmartbear.com/compass/](https://longform.asmartbear.com/compass/)

### المحاور والأفكار الجوهرية للمقال:
1. **الفكرة الأساسية:** البوصلة الاستراتيجية ليست خريطة تفصيلية: كيف تقود وسط الضباب تمثل إحدى الركائز الفكرية التي يركز عليها جيسون كوهين في بناء الشركات التقنية المستدامة.
2. **الواقع العملي:** تجنب الأخطاء الشائعة والقرارات العاطفية المتسرعة، والاعتماد على مقاييس دقيقة تفصل بين الضجيج والحقيقة الميدانية.
3. **التوصية الاستشارية:** إذا كنت تواجه هذا التحدي في شركتك، يمكنك استشارة جيسون كوهين عبر نافذة الشات الذكي لتطبيق هذه المبادئ على نموذج عملك الخاص.

# A compass is not a map

> **Executive Summary:** A compass tells you which way is north, not whether you should be heading north. So what is the map to startup success?
> **Original URL:** [https://longform.asmartbear.com/compass/](https://longform.asmartbear.com/compass/)

![Two desks - one reads 'Security Desk', other reads 'Insecurity Desk.'](https://public.asmartbear.com/books/anthology/chapters/compass/d1505print-simple.png)

An entrepreneur confided in me recently that he didn't like [Lean Startup](https://longform.asmartbear.com/lean-startup-ballad/). He asked whether I thought that was OK, or whether is he not cut out to build a startup. "Elon didn't use Lean Startup to build Tesla." True, but you're not manufacturing cars. Still, it's also true that before Lean Startup most startups failed, and after Lead Startup most startups failed, so is it really necessary?

A founder uses "37signals does it" to justify every decision, even if he can't explain why, or why it applies to his situation, or whether 37signals did that when they were still his size.

A startup pitch-deck boasts about the mistakes they've made because of the lessons they've learned. But the lessons haven't resulted in paying customers, so is there value in that experience? Is it time to hang up the towel, or does this knowledge and introspection position them for rocket ship growth?

A compass is not a map. A compass tells you **which way is north, not whether you should be heading north**.

There's no map in startups despite even [me saying there might be](https://longform.asmartbear.com/product-market-fit-formula/). There are examples, but for every startup that landed 300 customers from their TechCrunch launch article there's another which got 0; for every startup that built loyalty on Twitter there's one with 20% month-over-month revenue growth that has yet to reserve their Twitter handle; for every startup ascribing their success to spectacular design, there's another successful one who has [never hired a designer](https://longform.asmartbear.com/design/) (and it shows).

Indeed, for every piece of advice `P`, there are many successes and failures of startups who did `P`, and also many successes and failures of startups that did the opposite of `P`. The 2x2 diagram is full. So what does that tell us about `P`?

The trouble with "rules" in startups, besides the [inherent survivor bias](https://longform.asmartbear.com/survivor-bias/), is that **by definition the successful ones are anomalies**. Statistics show trends, but **trends don't predict outliers**.

What *does* predict an outlier? Nothing. A successful startup [looks a lot like an unsuccessful startup](https://longform.asmartbear.com/chaos-at-start/) at the start. [Statistics can't be used](https://longform.asmartbear.com/fallacies/) to determine the outcome of the individual case (not just in startups, but anywhere).

Don't confuse startup frameworks or dogma or the origin story of your favorite startup with laws. You have to find your own way, using all this startup advice for sparks of inspiration, for brainstorming, as a candy store where every item has its own merits but you must pick and choose what to put into your basket. Decide [what makes you the best version](https://longform.asmartbear.com/bad-advice/) of who [you already are](https://longform.asmartbear.com/pivot-points/).

There's no map. That's OK. Keep moving.
