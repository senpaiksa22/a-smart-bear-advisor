# 10 things I've never heard a successful startup founder say

> **Executive Summary:** Please stop repeating these ideas! This isn't how successful companies work.
> **Original URL:** [https://longform.asmartbear.com/successful-founders-dont-say/](https://longform.asmartbear.com/successful-founders-dont-say/)

![I started out under a bridge, moved to online commenting, but then I got into patents and everything changed.](https://longform.asmartbear.com/successful-founders-dont-say/e9e0d591.png)

* [I built this software for myself](https://longform.asmartbear.com/scratched-my-own-itch/), and then it turned out a million people wanted it *exactly* how I *originally* envisioned it.
* After hiring a few people, being the CEO became a lot easier, and I was able to focus on high-level strategic plans instead of fighting fires.
* I wish we had spent less time [talking to prospective customers](https://longform.asmartbear.com/customer-development/) before [designing interfaces](https://longform.asmartbear.com/design/) and writing code.
* The decision of whether to form an LLC, S-Corp, or C-Corp made a significant difference in my startup's success.
* [Selling the company](https://longform.asmartbear.com/rich-vs-king-sold-company/) was an easy decision, and everyone in the company was on the same page.
* We're such good actors, our first few customers [never knew](https://longform.asmartbear.com/customer-love/) we were a new company with no employees and buggy software.
* Thanks to a software patent we filed, we never had a serious competitor.
* Our most [effective marketing campaigns](https://longform.asmartbear.com/authentic-is-dead/) were the ones filled with buzzwords and non-specific claims.
* My lack of an MBA degree made building a company from scratch harder for me than for others.
* I wish I had spent more time reading and weighing the pros and cons of various philosophies instead of **just jumping in and doing what I thought was morally and financially sensible.**

Just get started, talk to potential customers, and ship stuff.
