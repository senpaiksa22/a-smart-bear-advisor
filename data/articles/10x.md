# Laws of 10x found everywhere
### رؤية وتجربة: Laws of 10x found everywhere

> **الملخص التنفيذي:** مقالة استراتيجية معمقة من جيسون كوهين ضمن محور 'استراتيجية الأعمال والنمو' تتناول أبعاد 'رؤية وتجربة: Laws of 10x found everywhere'، وتفكك الآليات والدروس المستفادة: We're often told that some things are 10x better than others. Is that really how things work? If so, we'd better act like it.
> **المحور الأساسي:** استراتيجية الأعمال والنمو
> **رابط المقال الأصلي:** [https://longform.asmartbear.com/10x/](https://longform.asmartbear.com/10x/)

![Cartoon 569f346d](https://longform.asmartbear.com/10x/97871f87.png)

We love stories more than science. A good story causes us to [see patterns in noise](https://longform.asmartbear.com/pattern-seeking-fallacy/).

Maybe that's the case with the idea that all manner of startup phenomena conform to a "10x Rule." It is really that common?

Yes, or true enough that it's an excellent rule of thumb that should be assumed until proven false.  Here's a slew of examples:

1. [Business models change based on pricing](https://longform.asmartbear.com/pricing-determines-your-business-model/)  in roughly 10x increments
2. [Great people are more than 10x better than average](https://longform.asmartbear.com/imbalanced-people/).
3. Winning products have at least one attribute  [that's 10x better than all competitors](https://bothsidesofthetable.com/your-product-needs-to-be-10x-better-than-the-competition-to-win-here-s-why-6168bab60de7)  (and it's  [not necessarily design](https://longform.asmartbear.com/design/), though it [can be](https://longform.asmartbear.com/creativity-over-optimization/)).
4. VCs  [must invest in 10x winners](https://medium.com/swlh/one-reason-you-re-not-being-funded-vc-math-explained-73d87d35a383), pushing every one of their portfolio companies to seek that outcome, even if it means increased risk and sub-optimal cash utilization.
5. [Companies do not naturally 10x their size or quality](https://longform.asmartbear.com/lindy-effect/); that achievement requires intentional focus and investment.
6. For multi-product companies, often one product generates more revenue than all the rest combined. (For Smart Bear and 37Signals, the largest  [was 10x larger than all the others combined](https://longform.asmartbear.com/complex-decisions/)!)
7. SaaS returns in a market are a power law; winner can take 10x the profits of the next.  *Examples:* eBay ≥ 10 × Etsy; Google ≥ 10 × Bing; Facebook ≥ 10 × Twitter; Salesforce ≥ 10 × [rest of market]
8. [Your best marketing channel](https://longform.asmartbear.com/one-channel/)  is often 10x the productivity of the next-best.  True of content-marketing  also.
9. [Scale is 10x harder](https://longform.asmartbear.com/scale-rare/)  than operating at small scale.  (Because complexity is non-linear.)
10. 1/10th of customers are likely (gross-margin) unprofitable. That doesn't mean you should "fire" them. But it's a calculation worth understanding, and a phenomenon worth measuring. And sometimes, changing your policy or product to reduce that set, is a quick and strategic way to generate profit.
11. 1/10th of customers generate 10x more support calls than the rest.  Some of that is OK, but some of that might be a signal about your product, or about your customers, or about those customers.
12. $77.4 billion of Warren Buffett's $77.7 billion net worth was accumulated after his 50th birthday. _(HT [@morganhousel](https://twitter.com/morganhousel/status/899440521294336001))_
13. One or two  [decisions or principles will be 10x more important than all others combined](https://longform.asmartbear.com/complex-decisions/).
14. One article or tweet [outperforms all the rest by 10x](https://twitter.com/_michaellin/status/1653282336451223552?s=46&t=mnlAhEmLkMKMeX_fkP_pzw):

![startup founder graph of growth](https://longform.asmartbear.com/10x/a367d5f9.png)

Things aren't always 10x, and some things have a 10x property for years  [but can evolve until there isn't another 10x](https://longform.asmartbear.com/another-10x/).

Still, it's useful to assume there is a 10x truth, and spend your time seeking that truth, rather than [worrying about](https://longform.asmartbear.com/worry/)  and fiddling with one hundred variables, almost none of which will turn out to be important.
